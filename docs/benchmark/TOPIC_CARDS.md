# Topic Cards — Benchmark Sessions (S0-01)

One card per clip. Each card spreads the required content from STT_BENCHMARK_PLAN §3.3 so the full
set covers the composition targets in §3.2. All names, companies, and numbers are **invented** —
never use real customers or real financial data.

**Every card also requires:** a sync clap at the start, at least one interruption (2+ people), one
10-second pause, one clear decision, and two action items with an owner and a date.
